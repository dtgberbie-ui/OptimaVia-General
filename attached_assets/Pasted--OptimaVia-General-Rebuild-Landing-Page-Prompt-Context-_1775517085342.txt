I have an existing landing page at optimavia.io that was built for a job board / two-sided marketplace. I have pivoted the product. OptimaVia General is now a **small business operations platform** — it helps businesses manage their teams, dispatch jobs, track finances, and run day-to-day operations from one app. It is NOT a job board or hiring platform anymore.

I need you to **replace the current landing page** with a new one that reflects the new product. Keep the same tech stack and project structure. Only change the landing/homepage content and design.

---

## What the product actually does now

OptimaVia General gives small businesses a single app to:
- **Assign and track jobs** — managers create jobs, assign them to workers, track status in real time
- **Dispatch field teams** — workers see assigned jobs on their phone, tap an address to navigate via Google Maps
- **Track revenue and expenses** — log income and costs, see profit at a glance
- **Photo accountability** — employees take check-in/check-out photos for proof of service and asset tracking
- **Calculate product costs** — businesses that make products can build recipes, calculate cost per unit, and set smart pricing
- **Manage their team** — add employees, assign roles, see who's working on what

The app serves different business types by toggling modules on/off. A field service company sees job dispatch features. A product-based business sees costing and pricing features. Both get the core financial tracking and team management.

## Design Requirements

- **Keep the same brand color scheme** as the current site (dark navy primary, clean whites and light grays)
- **Mobile-first responsive** — must look great on phones since that's how most users will access it
- **Remove ALL references to:**
  - "I'm an Employer" / "I'm a Worker" buttons
  - Job board language (candidates, hiring, matching, outreach, talent)
  - Fit & Reliability Score
  - Smart Matching
  - Instant Outreach
  - Any two-sided marketplace language
- **No mention of AI or algorithms** — keep it simple and practical. These are small business owners, not tech people.
- **Icons** should be simple outline-style (Lucide or Heroicons style). Use the same icon style as the current site.
- **Animations:** Subtle fade-in on scroll for sections. Nothing flashy.
- **The page should feel professional but approachable** — not corporate, not startup-y. Think "reliable tool for real businesses."
